## 原文

**Why Small Satellites Need Better Clocks**

A small satellite can carry a surprisingly good antenna these days. What it usually cannot carry is a good clock.

Cheap crystal oscillators drift by a few milliseconds a day, which is enough to spoil precise positioning.

One fictional team in this story corrects the drift in software, using a short time signal from the ground on every pass.

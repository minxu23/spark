## 小结

**用一台旧手机定时拍照，就能画出面团发酵的曲线**

- 背景：作者想知道面团在不同室温下发酵多快。
- 方法：旧手机每十分钟拍一张，按面团高度算体积。
- 结果：室温每高三度，发酵时间大约缩短四分之一。

## 原文

**Measuring Bread Rise with a Cheap Camera**

I wanted to know how fast my dough rises at different room temperatures, so I pointed an old phone at a glass jar and took a photo every ten minutes.

Measuring the height of the dough in each photo gave me a simple curve. Warmer rooms gave steeper curves, which is what you would expect, but the size of the effect surprised me.

The whole setup cost nothing except a jar and a piece of tape.

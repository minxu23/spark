## 文字记录

Most data pipelines in small teams are scripts that grew, um, That is fine, as long as they can be rerun.

Write every intermediate result to disk, so any single step can be run again on its own.

Every dependency is a future upgrade you will have to do, um, Prefer the standard library.

Write error messages for the person who gets woken up at three in the morning.

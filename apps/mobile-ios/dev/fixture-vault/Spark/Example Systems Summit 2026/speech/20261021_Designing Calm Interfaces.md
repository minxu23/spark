## 演讲稿

Good interfaces feel calm. Calm does not mean empty; it means nothing is asking for attention without a reason.

> 好的界面让人觉得安心。安心不等于空，而是没有任何东西在无缘无故地抢你的注意力。

Start with the three things people do most often, and hide everything else one tap away.

> 先找出大家最常做的三件事，其余的都放到点一下才出现的地方。

Notifications should be rare and accurate. If two alerts mean the same thing, merge them.

> 提醒要少而准。如果两条提醒说的是同一件事，就把它们合成一条。

Use motion to explain a change, never to attract the eye.

> 动画只用来解释发生了什么变化，不要用来吸引目光。

### 🗣️ Ada Wren

So Theo, where do you even start when somebody hands you a radio that's been in an attic for forty years?

### 🗣️ Theo Park

Power first, always. Check the plug, the fuse, the switch. You'd be surprised how many dead radios just need a new fuse.

### 🗣️ Ada Wren

And after that?

### 🗣️ Theo Park

Capacitors. They dry out with age. But discharge them before you touch anything, that part is not optional.
